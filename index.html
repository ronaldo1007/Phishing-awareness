STEP 1

What's a phishing scam?

Phishing is when someone pretends to be a company or person you trust — like a bank, a store, or even a friend — to trick you into giving up private info like your password, or clicking a bad link.
STEP 2

Spot the red flags

Feels rushed
"Act now or lose your account!" Real companies rarely give you minutes to respond.
Too good to be true
A free prize that just needs your info first? That's the trick, not the treat.
Stranger danger
Someone you don't know, asking for your password? Big warning sign.
Weird links
Misspelled or strange-looking web addresses usually aren't the real site.
